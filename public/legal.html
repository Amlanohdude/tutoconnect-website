TutoConnect operates strictly as a digital aggregator and matchmaking platform connecting students with independent tutors.

🛡️ Part 2: Privacy Policy (Lean DPDP Act Compliance)

Data Collection & Use

We collect necessary data (names, masked IDs, locations, subjects) solely to facilitate matches between tutors and students. We do not sell this data to third parties.

Minors' Data & Parental Consent

By registering a minor, the parent or lawful guardian provides verifiable consent for data processing under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Accounts created directly by minors without parental consent will be deleted immediately upon discovery.

Data Storage & Identity Document Safeguards (Aadhaar)

For regulatory compliance and safety, TutoConnect strictly requires tutors to upload 'Masked Aadhaar' (with only the last 4 digits visible).

Private Storage Protection: We store uploaded verification documents securely in private, encrypted cloud storage solely for internal screening. Uploaded identity documents are never made publicly visible on tutor profiles or anywhere on the platform.

Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

TutoConnect uses third-party advertising companies, such as Google AdMob, to serve ads within the mobile application. These companies may use cookies, mobile advertising IDs (e.g., Google Advertising ID / AAID), and device identifiers to collect non-personally identifiable information about your visits to this and other apps in order to provide advertisements about goods and services of interest to you.

User Rights, Account Deletion & Data Erasure

TutoConnect provides full control over your personal data. Users can request the complete deletion of their account and associated personal data at any time through either of the following methods:

  • In-App Self Service: Open TutoConnect ➔ Go to Settings ➔ Account ➔ Delete Account to instantly initiate account closure and data erasure.
  • Email Request: Send an email from your registered email address to tutoconnect.support@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request". Requests are processed within 24–48 hours.
Data Retention & Deletion Scope: Upon account deletion, all personal data—including your name, email, phone number, profile photo, subject preferences, and uploaded verification documents—is permanently purged from our database and storage. Non-identifiable aggregate logs may be retained solely to comply with applicable statutory legal obligations under Indian law.
